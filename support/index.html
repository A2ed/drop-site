drop. Support

Last updated: May 31, 2026

drop. is a private place to save links, notes, images, products, research, and ideas before they disappear.

What drop. does

  • Saves links, notes, images, and ideas
  • Organizes saved items into collections
  • Adds tags, favorites, and archive status
  • Searches saved drops locally
  • Supports saving from Safari and other apps with the iOS share sheet
  • Supports local AI search when on-device intelligence is available

Saving from Safari

Open the iOS share sheet from Safari or another app, choose drop., review the save sheet, then tap Drop. If the item does not appear immediately, open drop. and check the Drops tab.

Collections

Collections help group related drops, such as house ideas, gifts, school links, travel plans, or project research.

Troubleshooting

  • If a shared item does not appear, reopen drop. so it can import pending share-extension items.
  • If link previews are missing, the source website may not provide preview metadata or image access.
  • If local AI answers seem incomplete, add notes, tags, or collection names to the relevant drops and try again.

Privacy

drop. is private by default and does not require a separate account. Read the privacy policy.
